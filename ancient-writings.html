How large is the surviving Greek corpus?

The collection above is a curated starting catalog, not every extant ancient Greek text. Some works survive only in fragments; a multi-book treatise may be counted as one work, and collections such as Moralia or Parallel Lives contain many separate pieces. Ancient writing dates are often uncertain, so the date column uses a broad author/activity date, not a verified publication date for each title.

For scale, the open Open Greek Corpus catalogs about 3,910 works by 1,501 authors, spanning antiquity and later Greek literature; Eulogikon indexes about 4,060 Greek-language works including fragments and late antiquity. Different corpus boundaries and counting rules give different totals. Your progress percentage above applies only to this starter list, not the entire surviving Greek-language tradition.

Reading sources: MIT Internet Classics Archive (English), Perseus / Scaife (Greek and translations), Open Greek Corpus, and Eulogikon (Greek originals). Individual “Find text” links search the author's and title's public texts rather than promising a specific edition is available.

Scope: Greek-language authors through late antiquity, including some writers under Roman rule. Attributions such as Prometheus Bound, Rhesus, and Epinomis are disputed. Fragmentary and disputed items are explicitly marked where applicable. This first catalog is a navigational index, not a critical bibliography.
